A Letter to My Kids

The years Beaumont spent under investigation — and how the city found its way back.

My kids were 10 and 13 when this began. They knew their dad went to a lot of meetings. They didn’t know that some of those meetings were with investigators from the FBI and the Riverside County District Attorney’s office, or that the city they were growing up in was the subject of one of the largest municipal-corruption cases in the region’s history. They’re adults now. I’m writing it all down for them — and posting it here, a chapter a week, for anyone in Beaumont who wants to understand what happened and how far the city has come.
